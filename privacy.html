Privacy Notice

Effective July 20, 2026

Vertex is the public brand of HM Vertex Group. This notice explains how information submitted through the Vertex website and benchmark-request form is handled.

Information we collect

When you request a benchmark, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, company, work email address, role or category, and the priority SKUs or spend categories you submit. Our website and hosting providers may also receive routine technical information sent by your browser, such as IP address, device type, browser type, and request timestamps.

How we use information

We use submitted information to review and route your benchmark request, contact you about next steps, prepare the requested benchmark process, maintain internal lead and follow-up records, protect the website from misuse, and improve the reliability of the intake workflow.

How information is shared

We may share information with service providers that support website hosting, form delivery, email delivery, storage, security, or related business operations. We do not sell personal information. Supplier identities, supplier contacts, direct pricing paths, negotiated terms, internal comparison logic, and sourcing strategy are not disclosed through the public website and remain subject to controlled access and applicable agreements.

Retention

We retain benchmark-request and related business records for as long as reasonably needed to respond, operate the benchmark process, maintain business records, resolve disputes, protect the service, and meet applicable legal or contractual obligations.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, subject to legal, contractual, security, and recordkeeping requirements. Use the benchmark form on the Vertex home page and state that your message concerns privacy.

Current site scope

The public Vertex launch site does not provide customer accounts, checkout, payment processing, an open supplier directory, supplier discovery, or automatic supplier switching.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website and operating process change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the public benchmark form on the Vertex home page and identify the request as a privacy inquiry.
